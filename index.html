A simple cooking rhythm.

Use each cooking day to support the next meal, reduce repetition, and keep the week easy to manage.

Cook night

  1. Make 4 servings for ordinary recipes.
  2. Serve 2 that night.
  3. Package the other 2 before everybody starts “sampling.”
  4. Assign them to the next day.

Batch night

  1. Use a 6–8 serving crockpot meal.
  2. Eat it once as written.
  3. Remix it once: tacos, bowls, pasta, potatoes, or sandwiches.
  4. Freeze anything beyond two nights.

Weekend rhythm

  1. Cook a fresh meal Saturday.
  2. Cook Sunday with leftovers in mind.
  3. Use Sunday’s extra servings for Monday.
  4. Start Tuesday without touching a skillet.
